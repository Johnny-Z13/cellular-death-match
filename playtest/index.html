Alpha Build 1Dr. E has friends to find.

Dr. E is now a living colony of cells who talks you through the lab. Cultures that you separate, feed and hold together merge into one organism, and each new one is a friend for his collection: thirty-two in all.

New in this build

  • Dr. E, made of cells. A friendly colony face with expressions, who speaks every line in his own mumble voice while the words appear.
  • Merging. Get two or more cultures alone together, feed them, and hold them. A ring fills, and they lock into one creature that moves as one.
  • Thirty-two friends. Strains are common, merges are rare, and five very rare ones only turn up in long play. Open Friends (top right) to see them all.
  • The rescue. Your first of each friend rises out of the dish with a fanfare.
  • Full-screen desktop. On a wide screen the dish fills the window and the menus float over it. Press F for the dish alone.

Known in this build

  • Dr. E’s voice is a first draft and has not been tuned by ear yet.
  • The tutorial teaches strains first; merging is a bonus invitation at the end of Trial 1 for now.
  • Your progress lives in this browser. A different browser or device starts fresh.
  • Phones on their side use the older side-rail layout.
  • The app icon is an old placeholder.

Getting started

On a computer +

Click Play now. It runs in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox; nothing to install. Press F during a trial for the dish alone, full screen, and F or Esc to bring the menus back.

On a phone or tablet +

Open this page on your phone and tap Play now. For a full-screen app with its own icon, add it to your home screen:

  1. iPhone and iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Play the game page itself (/play), not this one, when you add it.

Rescue your first friend

  1. Find clear agar, away from other cultures.
  2. Plant two Swarmlet eggs side by side.
  3. Drop one Nutrient on them and watch the ring fill. Keep others away for four seconds.

Every friend has a hint in Friends. Some only lock in Water or Salt.
