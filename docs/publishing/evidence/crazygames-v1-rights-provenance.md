## Provider and source notes

- All 21 bundled MP3 files were produced by the repository's ElevenLabs Sound
  Effects script using `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`. Exact prompts, parameters and
  hashes are preserved in `public/audio/generated/manifest.json`. Commercial
  clearance remains conditional on confirming that the generating ElevenLabs
  account was on an eligible paid plan at the time of generation. Repository
  addition history is anchored by commits `bf2f7db`, `4733654`, `1136308`, and
  `05fffab` (2026-06-06 through 2026-06-10).
- The title and eight chimera images were generated through Google's Gemini API
  (`gemini-2.5-flash-image`, labelled “Nano Banana” in the original scripts).
  Exact prompts are preserved in the generator scripts.
- Professor E. Mergent and the app-icon master were produced with OpenAI's
  built-in image generation tool. Their prompts, source relationships and exact
  output hashes are recorded in repository evidence.
- The onboarding pointer was inherited from Brickinetic. It was generated with
  OpenAI image generation using two user-supplied screenshots as gesture and
  silhouette references. Rights to those reference images have not been
  established, so this exact file remains `review-required`.
- The browser currently loads Michroma, IBM Plex Mono and Newsreader from Google
  Fonts. They are not bundled media and therefore are outside the digest table;
  all three families are published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. For a
  portal submission, locally bundle the required files and licence notices or
  obtain explicit approval for the runtime dependency.
