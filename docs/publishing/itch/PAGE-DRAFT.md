## Page body

Welcome to Dr. E. Mergent’s laboratory.

Plant an egg, feed the culture, and see what it becomes. Mix reagents, bring different strains together, and discover the strange life hiding in your Petri dish.

Five guided trials introduce the lab. Then the Open Lab lets you choose new studies, build your collection, and experiment with a living ecosystem.

- Grow cultures with distinct shapes and behaviour.
- Discover reactions, new strains, hybrids and merged friends.
- Collect discoveries in your Notebook and use discovered strains in later trials.
- Find a balance between hunter and prey, or watch the dish collapse.

**Controls:** Click or tap a tool, then click or tap the dish. Egg selects a culture to plant; Nutrient feeds it. More tools unlock as you complete trials. Open Options to adjust sound or leave a trial.

**Saving:** Progress is stored in this browser on this device. Completed trials and discoveries are kept; interrupted dishes restart fresh. Clearing browser data removes your progress. If browser storage is unavailable, the game warns you and allows temporary play; reloading then loses that session’s progress.

A cellular ecosystem game by Z13 Labs, inspired by cellular automata and the Cellular Potts Model.
