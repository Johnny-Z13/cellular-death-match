# Dr. E. Mergent portrait record

## Pixel portrait

`public/art/professor/dr-e-mergent-pixel.png` replaces the painted portrait
everywhere Dr. E appears: the welcome and method introductions, the HUD, and
the onboarding pointer. Since 2026-10-14 he is not on the title or the store
cover; the live dish carries the brand. Johnny asked on 2026-10-14 for a
professor that looks less AI-generated, in the same visual family as the
genome sprites.

- Installed: 2026-10-14
- Grid: 64 × 80 cells, written at 24× as a 1536 × 1920 PNG with a transparent
  background
- SHA-256: `c50b394b186536a17d698adc6a9a65b2295bc0b67c56dfbda050f0ef6bb3ac75`
- Source: `scripts/draw-professor-pixel.mjs`. Shapes and individual pixels are
  placed in code, and no image model is involved. The code was written with
  Claude Code. Re-running the script reproduces the file byte for byte.
- Design: the name and voice are kept, along with a lean face, a white lab
  coat, silver hair swept back into a crest, and a smile slightly too wide. A
  brass jeweller's loupe magnifies one teal eye. Violet nitrile gloves hold a
  glowing dish, and a teal colony is pinned to the lapel as a brooch. The
  palette is the game's own: bio-teal, amber and violet.
- Distribution status: provider-declared; named human approval is still required

## Retired painted portraits

The earlier painted portraits (OpenAI image generation) are no longer in the
repo: the concept `professor-emergent-concept-v1.png`, the production crop
`professor-emergent-v1.png` and the high-resolution master
`professor-emergent-v2.png`. They and their full generation record were
archived on 2026-10-14 to
`~/Projects/Archive/cellular-death-match-legacy-art-2026-10/`, and commit
`1667513` is the last that contains them.
