## Calls made for Johnny

1. **Who gets the wide layout:** landscape screens at least 520 px tall and 1.25× wider than tall. That covers desktops, laptops, Steam Deck and landscape tablets. Phones on their side and nearly square windows keep the classic layout. `?layout=wide|classic` forces either one (`DISH_LAYOUT_TUNING`, `src/ui/dishShape.ts`).
2. **The wide dish is 213 × 120 sites,** within 0.2% of the square dish's 25,600, so pace and balance carry over. A dish keeps its shape for its whole life.
3. **The bars float over the dish ("bleed"), not beside it ("inset").**
4. **Dr. E's look: the colony direction.** He is cells of several strains (his friends' colours), with white eyes, a glint, pink cheeks and a darker core. Merge, blob and loupe are still at `/drelook.html`. To switch, change `DR_E_DIRECTION` in `src/content/drETuning.ts`.
5. **Dr. E's voice:** Bubble imp, pitch +3, pace 1.1, weird 0.6. This is the plan's starting point and has not been tuned by ear; tune it at `/voicelab.html`.
6. **Dr. E speaks only titles.** The coach's titles are short spoken lines; the bodies stay as captions, so he never babbles a paragraph.
7. **The merge rule:** isolated (firm contact with nothing else), fed in the form's field, held for 4 s. Forming groups stick and hold off dividing; locked forms divide as normal. From the census: `docs/v1/merge-census.md`.
8. **Bond strength 0.95** of surface tension. Above about 1.0, partners started growing into each other; below 0.8 they drifted apart (adhesion sweep).
9. **Firm contact means at least 6 touching pixel pairs,** so a neighbour brushing past does not spoil a forming merge.
10. **The twelve rares and five very rares,** including Johnny's two reds around a green as **The Sandwich** (#026). It is the triangle: a live probe showed a strict chain (Splitter between Snipers that do not touch) closes into the triangle under the bonds, which is also what Johnny saw on the title (`af423e9`).
11. **No new Trial 2 tonight.** Inserting a trial shifts every per-trial table and about 40 tests, and the census shows Bloom Buddy rarely holds in Trial 2's dish. Instead, Trial 1's result invites the merge as a bonus. A First Merge trial with its own calm dish is the next step.
12. **Commons keep the existing strain reveal;** the new ta-da is for merged friends.
13. **Faces render at 30 fps with at most 6 backing pixels per cell.** Four live faces at 3× density made the phone browser tests time out.
