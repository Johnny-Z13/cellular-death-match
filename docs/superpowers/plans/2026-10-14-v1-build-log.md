## Calls made for Johnny

1. **Who gets the wide layout.** Landscape screens 520 px or taller: desktops, laptops, Steam Deck and landscape tablets. Phones on their side (around 390 px tall) keep today's side-rail layout, because the floating bars left too little dish (see the experiment's 844 × 390 shot). `?layout=wide` or `?layout=classic` forces either one. The threshold is `DISH_LAYOUT_TUNING.wideMinHeightPx` in `src/ui/dishShape.ts`.
2. **The wide dish is 213 × 120 sites.** That is within 0.2% of the square dish's 25,600, so pace and balance carry over. A dish keeps its shape for its whole life; only its framing follows the window.
3. **Bars float over the dish ("bleed"), not beside it ("inset").** The experiment compared both. Bleed is the full-screen feel Johnny asked for, and translucent glass keeps the cultures under the bars visible. Inset made the dish about 15% smaller at 1440 × 900.
