## Harness cadence

- Pull requests: unit suite, build, responsive smoke, persistence path, one full
  five-Trial journey, and one Open Lab journey.
- Scheduled/nightly: repeated save/discovery soak, all Open Lab objective
  scenarios, all responsive projects, and journey timing capture.
- Manual release check: physical iOS/Android touch, audio unlock/mute, haptics,
  VoiceOver/TalkBack announcements, and interruption by browser/OS chrome.
  Automation timing is reported as harness throughput, never asserted as human
  play length.

No failure may be dismissed solely as “flaky” without a reproduced cause or a
bounded quarantine with an owner and removal condition.
